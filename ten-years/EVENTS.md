# Management-event verification register

Checked 4 November 2026 against official CITES documents accessible through indexed original text. Selected records, not an exhaustive legal history. Some direct PDF downloads were interrupted by the approval service connection; no local PDF archive is claimed. The UI links the original documents and identifies the paragraphs/table used.

|---|---|---|
| 2017–2018 | Indonesia export ban | Indonesia's national-authority response, AC33 Doc. 14.3 (Rev. 1), Table 1, printed p. 62, reports bans under MAF 48/2016 and 5/2018. National exports, not global trade. Year precision; national instruments not independently inspected. |

| 2024 | Ecuador suspension recommendation | Notification 2024/043, paragraphs 1–3. Effective 11 March 2024; commercial specimens from Ecuador or with Ecuadorian origin. S. lewini explicitly included. Later Notification 2025/036 narrows the scope effective 25 February 2025, excluding S. lewini; no 2025 event is added to the decade. |

All URLs, dates, precision, scope and evidence notes are preserved in [the machine-readable register](dist/data/cites-management-events.json). The JS bundle mirrors it. Display years refer to occurrence/periods stated above, not assumed continuing legal status. A record appears with both reporting sides and all three fin forms because its legal/contextual scope is not a statistical product code or reporting side. This does not imply every record in those aggregate series is covered.

The graph remains global, across purposes, countries and direct/re-export trade. Country measures are contextual cards only: no policy line, weight threshold or inferred effect is added to the global curve. Annual trade data cannot identify shipments inside a short suspension, determine compliance or prove effectiveness. Retrospectively reported national measures are labelled separately from direct CITES decisions/notifications. Missing event cards do not mean no management measures occurred.

## Baseline and deliberately excluded claims

- Appendix II took effect on 14 September 2014, outside the displayed period. It is a baseline note, not a new 2015 event. Source: [CoP17 Doc. 56.1](https://cites.org/sites/default/files/eng/cop/17/WorkingDocs/E-CoP17-56-01.pdf).
- The wider Sphyrnidae listing in 2023 is not presented as a new listing of the already-listed S. lewini. Party reservations require separate treatment: [CITES reservations](https://cites.org/eng/app/reserve.php).
- Malaysia 2015–2017 zero-quota reports were not added because the original table's country/subnational coverage was not fully established in the accessible excerpt.
- AC33 recommendations (including Yemen's proposed zero quota) are not represented as implemented restrictions. Adoption, conditions and subsequent implementation must be verified separately.
- No fabricated exact day for year-only national histories; no inferred policy events in 2015, 2016, 2021 or 2022.
